What is the difference between Premium and Ultra Premium?

Premium is fresh, hand-sorted quality for everyday use. Ultra Premium is top-grade, jumbo, single-origin produce in signature packaging — ideal for gifting and connoisseurs.

Do you take bulk and corporate orders?

Absolutely. We offer special pricing on orders of 10 kg and above and can create custom hampers for corporate gifting.
